# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project. Be constructive, assume good intent, accept feedback gracefully, and focus disagreements on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project concerns, open a GitHub issue. For sensitive conduct matters, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Do not publish private or identifying information in an issue.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, and the safety of the community.
